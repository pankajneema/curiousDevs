## 1. Executive summary

CuriousDevs is an AI engineering and intelligent-systems studio. We research, engineer, and build technology for difficult real-world problems.

The company works across a connected continuum:

**Research -> Engineering -> Systems -> Products -> Real-world impact**

The near-term focus is production AI engineering: building AI-native products, improving existing AI systems, and creating the evaluation, security, deployment, and operating discipline required for reliable production use.

The longer-term direction extends toward intelligent systems, robotics, edge intelligence, advanced hardware, and DeepTech. Future capability should be presented as a direction under development, not as an unsupported current claim.

## 2. Positioning

### Category

AI engineering and intelligent-systems studio.

### One-line description

CuriousDevs helps teams build, fix, and scale serious AI systems for production.

### What we believe

A model call is not a production system. A dependable system also needs:

- Clear scope, ownership, and acceptance criteria.
- Grounded data, retrieval quality, and reliable context.
- Evaluation sets, regression checks, and measurable baselines.
- Security, permissions, guardrails, and data-flow discipline.
- Latency, cost, observability, deployment, and incident readiness.
- Documentation, handover, and a team that can operate the result.

### What we are not

- A generic software outsourcing shop.
- A prompt-only consultancy.
- A slide-deck AI consultancy.
- A prototype-only studio.
- A hype-driven AI brand.
- A DeepTech company by claim alone.

## 4. What CuriousDevs does

### Build

Design and engineer AI-native products and intelligent workflows:

- RAG applications, enterprise search, and knowledge copilots.
- AI agents, tools, APIs, memory, permissions, and workflow automation.
- Planner, executor, reviewer, and multi-agent orchestration.
- Multimodal product features and embedded intelligence.
- Backend systems, integrations, data pipelines, and application logic.
- Evaluation, security, deployment, documentation, and handover.

### Audit / Assessment

Create a clear view of an existing AI system before the next investment:

- Retrieval, grounding, answer quality, and hallucination review.
- Agent trajectory, tool-use, edge-case, and regression review.
- Prompt injection, access control, data leakage, and guardrail review.
- Latency, token usage, cost, reliability, and infrastructure review.
- Scorecard, failure map, priority order, and decision-ready next step.

### Fix

Diagnose root causes and improve reliability:

- Retrieval precision and recall.
- Agent QA and unsafe trajectories.
- Accuracy, latency, cost, and workflow failures.
- MCP, tool permissions, integrations, and data-flow risks.
- Before-and-after evidence for every meaningful change.

### Scale

Productionize AI systems so teams can operate them:

- Cloud, private, and VPC deployment.
- Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and release controls.
- Model routing, semantic caching, token attribution, and cost policies.
- Logs, traces, metrics, alerts, and incident runbooks.
- Security hardening, resilience, fallback behavior, and access governance.
- Documentation, training, QA, and ongoing engineering support.

## 5. Technology areas

These are four connected areas inside one intelligent-systems thesis.

### 01. AI Engineering

**Building intelligent software that works in production.**

LLMs, RAG, agents, automation, multimodal AI, evaluation, security, reliability, observability, infrastructure, and deployment.

Core question: how do we turn a model or workflow into a reliable, measurable system?

### 02. Intelligent Systems

**Moving intelligence closer to where perception and decisions happen.**

Computer vision, edge AI, embedded intelligence, sensors, real-time inference, decision systems, and on-device AI.

Core question: what should happen locally, within real latency, power, connectivity, and safety limits?

### 03. Robotics

**Extending intelligence into machines that perceive and act.**

Perception, planning, control, navigation, manipulation, autonomy, physical AI, and machine intelligence.

Core question: how do perception, planning, and control close the loop on a physical machine?

### 04. DeepTech

**Exploring deeper technologies that expand what intelligent systems can do.**

AI hardware, accelerators, specialized compute, AI-native devices, advanced sensors, intelligent machines, neurotechnology / BCI, and advanced computing.

DeepTech is the umbrella for the deeper physical and computational layers. Robotics, physical AI, autonomy, and embedded systems can be represented within this direction where appropriate.

## 6. Product directions

### Noema

**Intelligence that understands.**

Noema is the intelligence layer: a multimodal foundation for perception, representation, reasoning, planning, and action.

It is designed to connect:

**Text / image / audio / sensor context -> perception -> understanding -> reasoning -> planning -> action**

Noema focuses on grounded context, auditable tool use, uncertainty-aware decisions, measurable evaluation, and real-world task outcomes.

Key questions:

- What representation best connects language to the state of the world?
- How should uncertainty change a model's plan or request for help?
- Which evaluations demonstrate reliable understanding beyond a polished demo?

### Soma

**Intelligence, embodied.**

Soma is the embodiment layer and humanoid robotics direction: a physical platform with vision, depth, touch, balance, edge compute, dexterous control, and safe motion.

It is designed around:

**Sensors -> perception -> understand -> plan -> move -> act -> adapt**

Soma focuses on low-latency edge inference, perception-to-action budgets, hard safety boundaries, manipulation, autonomy, episode capture, and telemetry.

Key questions:

- How should a humanoid degrade safely when perception is uncertain?
- Which skills transfer across homes, workplaces, and changing objects?
- What latency budget is required from camera to hand?

### Noema x Soma

Noema helps systems understand. Soma gives that intelligence a body capable of acting in the physical world.

The relationship is explicit:

- Noema can propose.
- Soma can execute.
- Safety limits, permissions, fallbacks, and human override remain outside the unconstrained model path.
- Every deployment should produce evidence that improves the next iteration.

## 7. Research-to-product continuum

CuriousDevs uses a continuous operating model:

1. **Research** - explore the question and the possible solution space.
2. **Prototype** - validate the core loop and the riskiest assumption.
3. **Engineer** - turn the loop into a reliable, secure, testable system.
4. **Deploy** - put it into the context where value is created.
5. **Operate and evolve** - monitor, learn, improve, and document the next version.

Each stage should leave evidence for the next stage. A handoff is not complete until the next owner understands the system, its limits, and its operating path.

### Contact form behavior

The website contact form:

- Validates the visitor's name and email.
- Captures company, stage, problem, and context.
- Sends the message through the server-side SMTP action.
- Delivers to `CONTACT_EMAIL`.
- Uses the visitor's email as `Reply-To`.
- Shows a direct `mailto:` fallback when sending fails.

Never place SMTP credentials in client-side code or commit `.env.local`.

## 12. Decision rules

When adding new company or product content:

1. State whether it is current capability, active product direction, or future research.
2. Tie every diagram to a real system relationship.
3. Avoid invented customers, deployments, performance numbers, or research results.
4. Prefer measurable engineering language over generic AI marketing.
5. Keep the four technology areas connected rather than presenting them as unrelated businesses.
6. Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, and a clear fallback for every important action.
7. Keep the website, README, PDF, social banner, and product pages consistent with this document.
